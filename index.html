Zeka Kutusu - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Summary

Zeka Kutusu ("the app") is a collection of brain and board games (memory, sudoku, minesweeper, chess, backgammon, mancala and others). The app needs no account, email or phone number. The only personal detail it ever asks for is an optional nickname you type in during first launch, and that name never leaves your device. Your game progress, settings and language choice are stored only on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK (Google AdMob) collects to show ads.

Data stored on your device

The optional nickname, selected language, sound preference, daily play-streak count and each game's saved progress are stored in local files on your device (Android's app storage / PlayerPrefs). We do not receive or have access to this data. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded (opt-in, for in-game hints) ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Analytics and crash data

The app does not send analytics events to any server of its own.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you on any server, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy tools. To remove the optional nickname and all local data at any time, uninstall the app.
